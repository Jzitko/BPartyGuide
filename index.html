Recommended homebase bar

Ten Cat Tavern

It's a 10-minute walk from the front door, it has room for twelve, it has something to do besides drink, and nobody has to make a reservation to get in.

  • Walkable — straight shot down Ashland, no Ubers, no one gets stranded.
  • Two free pool tables — a bracket runs itself and keeps twelve guys occupied.
  • Big patio + cheap beer — spillover space when the bar fills up.
  • Open late — 2 a.m. Friday, 3 a.m. Saturday. It works as the meet-up and the fallback.
